# Design Resources

## Existing NEXUS design system

The deployed marketing site is the brand reference. The operational product inherits its precision, spatial language, typography, restrained signal palette, and micro-interaction quality—not its cinematic density or fictional capabilities.

## UI/UX Pro Max

Installed under `.agents/skills/ui-ux-pro-max`. Mandatory for:

- product-wide visual direction;
- new operational workflows;
- responsive and accessibility review;
- design-token changes.

## 21st.dev

Use 21st.dev to research high-quality interaction and component patterns. Before adopting a pattern:

1. confirm it supports the actual operational task;
2. adapt it to NEXUS tokens and accessibility rules;
3. review dependency, bundle, license, and maintenance impact;
4. avoid copying catalog code merely for novelty.

Previously reviewed patterns include interactive spatial networks and data-field visualizations. They are inspiration only.

## Approved references

- The current deployed NEXUS marketing site
- MapLibre examples for map interaction behavior
- WCAG 2.2 and WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices
- Official platform documentation

Avoid generic SaaS templates, excessive glassmorphism, decorative 3D in field workflows, and unsupported AI/telemetry visual language.
