## What is preserved

- Brand mark, typography direction, palette, spacing rhythm, and motion character
- Cinematic marketing 3D experience
- Scroll-driven spatial storytelling
- Existing responsive/mobile treatment
- Reusable motion easing and interaction patterns

## What remains marketing-only

- Cinematic 3D network scene
- Fictional asset counts and operational metrics
- Illustrative live telemetry/signals
- Predictive-maintenance narrative
- Broad energy/telecom/water/industrial positioning
- Scale and latency claims

These elements cannot be treated as implemented MVP capability.

## What will connect to the real product

- Sign-in and authenticated entry to `/app`
- Product and platform CTAs
- Real screenshots/data demonstrations generated by the backend
- Truthful inspection, finding, corrective-action, evidence, and map narratives

## What must not leak into product architecture

- Marketing-only hardcoded metrics
- Client-only fake operational state
- Three.js as an authenticated-product dependency
- Telemetry, prediction, digital-twin, or general FSM assumptions
- Marketing component structure as the operational design system

## Migration notes

- Keep marketing components in a route group separate from authenticated application components.
- Extract only durable tokens and low-level primitives into `packages/ui`.
- Replace unsupported claims before public MVP launch.
- Preserve a lightweight no-WebGL fallback.
