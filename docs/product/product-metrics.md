# Product Metrics

Metrics are derived from real tenant events and exclude demo/synthetic data unless explicitly filtered.

## Activation

- Organization created
- First active site and asset imported/created
- First template published and plan scheduled
- First inspection submitted

## Operational usage

- Due inspections completed on time
- Median assignment-to-submission time
- Open and overdue findings/actions by severity
- Verification cycle time

## Evidence quality

- Submitted runs satisfying required evidence
- Evidence finalization failure rate
- Reports successfully generated from immutable records

## Mobile/offline

- Assigned packages cached successfully
- Offline sessions completed and later synchronized
- Command conflict and photo retry rates

Targets are intentionally unset until design-partner baselines exist. Metrics must never be marketed as proven outcomes without evidence.
