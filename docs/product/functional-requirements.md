# Functional Requirements

## Purpose

This is the stable, implementation-level functional requirements catalogue. Each requirement is authoritative unless superseded by the product specification or an accepted ADR.

Priority uses **Must** and **Should**. Status remains **Planned** until the owning phase satisfies its acceptance criteria and mapped tests.
