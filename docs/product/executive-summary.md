# Executive Summary

## Purpose

Define the product boundary that all requirements, architecture, and implementation must support.

## Decision

NEXUS is a spatial-first inspection-to-action and operational-evidence platform for organizations managing distributed physical assets.

Its central value is the continuous, spatially linked chain:

`Asset → Inspection → Finding → Corrective Action → Evidence → Verification → History`

NEXUS helps an operator establish what existed, what was inspected, what was found, what action followed, and what evidence proves closure.

## Initial customer

The initial ICP is small-to-mid-sized distributed power and energy operations and maintenance organizations with roughly:

- 10–250 sites;
- 50–10,000 tracked assets;
- 5–100 operational users;
- recurring field inspections;
- fragmented spreadsheets, messages, photos, and files;
- intermittent field connectivity.

These ranges are product assumptions, not market-size claims.

## Product principle

> Know what was inspected. Know what was found. Know what was fixed. Prove it.
