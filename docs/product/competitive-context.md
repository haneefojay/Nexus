# Competitive Context

## Alternatives

- Spreadsheets and shared drives: flexible but weak on state, authorization, provenance, and offline reconciliation.
- Generic field-service management: strong dispatch/work orders, but often too broad for inspection-to-verification continuity.
- CMMS/EAM platforms: deep asset maintenance, but frequently heavy to deploy for smaller distributed operators.
- Monitoring/SCADA: strong live signals, but not a substitute for field inspection evidence and remediation workflow.

## Position

NEXUS is an inspection-led spatial operations system, not a general work-order suite, telemetry platform, or digital-twin simulator. Its wedge is a credible record connecting assets, recurring inspection, evidence, findings, corrective actions, and verification.

This positioning is a hypothesis to validate with design partners, not a claim of proven market superiority.
