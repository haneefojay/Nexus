# Value Proposition

NEXUS gives distributed energy O&M teams a spatial, evidence-backed operating picture of their infrastructure.

## Promise

Know what exists, what was inspected, what needs attention, who is acting, and whether the work was verified.

## Differentiation hypothesis

NEXUS joins seven things generic field tools often split apart: inspection coverage, structured condition capture, spatial context, evidence continuity, corrective-action continuity, verification, and historical traceability.

## Truth boundary

MVP intelligence is deterministic and derived from entered operational records. NEXUS does not claim live telemetry, AI diagnosis, or predictive maintenance.
