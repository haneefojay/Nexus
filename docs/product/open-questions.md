# Open Product Questions

Architecture and MVP engineering fundamentals are settled. Remaining questions require customer validation:

1. Will distributed power operators pay for a focused inspection-to-action platform instead of spreadsheets, messaging, or a broader FSM?
2. Is inspection/evidence the urgent recurring pain that justifies a dedicated product?
3. Is the strongest first market Africa or a global distributed-power ICP?
4. How much offline capability do actual customers require?
5. Which report and evidence formats do owners, clients, regulators, and QA teams require?
6. Are existing FSM products already good enough for the target customer?
7. Should organization-based pricing primarily scale with active sites, assets, retention, or reports?

Do not fabricate answers or customer evidence.
