# Problem

Distributed energy O&M teams manage sites and assets across wide areas while operational evidence is fragmented across spreadsheets, chat, maps, email, and paper. Managers cannot reliably answer what is due, what was inspected, what failed, who owns remediation, or whether remediation was verified.

## Root causes

- Physical assets are spatially distributed and inconsistently identified.
- Inspection templates, schedules, evidence, and follow-up are disconnected.
- Field connectivity is unreliable.
- Generic work-management tools do not preserve asset, location, inspection, and evidence context.

## Product response

NEXUS creates one tenant-isolated system of record from site and asset inventory through inspection, finding, corrective action, verification, and report. It does not ingest live telemetry or predict failures in MVP.
