# Out of Scope

The MVP does not include:

## Infrastructure and integrations

- IoT, SCADA, live telemetry, device gateways, satellite/drone processing
- GIS synchronization, OGC federation, ArcGIS integration
- Digital twins or operational 3D

## AI and prediction

- LLM assistants, automated diagnosis, computer vision
- anomaly or failure prediction
- predictive maintenance

## General field service and enterprise operations

- route/dispatch optimization, workforce scheduling, timesheets
- labor costing, inventory, parts, procurement
- estimates, invoicing, billing, payments, subscriptions
- CRM, ERP, EAM, CMMS, or full FSM behavior

## Enterprise and mobile

- SAML, SCIM, custom RBAC, multi-region residency, private networking
- native iOS or Android applications

## Analytics

- advanced BI, predictive analytics, or cross-organization benchmarking

Marketing visuals may illustrate future possibilities but must not present these items as implemented product capability.
