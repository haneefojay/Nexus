# Product Thesis

Distributed physical assets are commonly managed through disconnected registers, checklists, messages, PDFs, photos, email, and generic task tools.

The critical failure is not simply lack of visibility. Operators cannot reliably connect:

`asset → condition → inspection → inspector → finding → action → evidence → closure`

NEXUS creates that operational chain and keeps it spatially anchored.

## Differentiation hypothesis

NEXUS is the inspection and evidence layer, not the entire operational stack. It focuses on:

- inspection coverage;
- structured condition capture;
- spatial context;
- evidence continuity;
- corrective-action continuity;
- verification;
- historical traceability.

This is a commercial hypothesis requiring validation. Do not claim competitors lack these capabilities or that customers have validated willingness to pay.

## Primary job to be done

> I need to know whether my distributed assets were actually inspected, what the field team found, what requires attention, whether corrective work happened, and have evidence supporting that conclusion.
